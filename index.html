Idle Rats — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 6 October 2026
Studio: SkaRift ("we", "us")
Contact: skarift.support@proton.me

This policy explains what information the mobile game Idle Rats ("the game") collects, why, who handles it, and the choices you have.

1. What we collect

Account and gameplay data

  • Player ID. When you first open the game it creates an anonymous account with Unity Authentication. We do not ask for your name, email address or a password. The account gets a random Player ID.
  • Google Play Games link (optional, Android). If Google Play Games is available, the game links your anonymous account to your Google Play Games account so that your progress comes back after a reinstall or on a new phone. We receive an identifier for that link. We do not see your Google password.
  • Game progress. Your characters, levels, currencies, stage progress, settings and other save data are stored on your device and backed up (encrypted) to Unity Cloud Save.
  • Profile. Your display name, avatar, frame, profile level and unlocked cosmetics are stored in Cloud Save and shown to other players where the game shows profiles.
  • Arena and CheddarCombs. Arena battles use snapshots of your party (display name, avatar, party and tier) that other players can fight. Rankings and tiers are stored on our servers.
  • Server time. The game asks our server for the current time to run daily resets and offline progress fairly.

Chat

  • Messages. In-game chat is text-only and runs through Unity Vivox. Messages you send in World chat are visible to other players in that channel, along with your display name, avatar and Arena tier.
  • Reports and moderation. If a message is reported, we store the reported message, who sent it and the reporter's note. Moderators can mute or ban accounts that break the rules.

Analytics (only if you agree)

  • On first launch the game asks whether you want to Share usage data. If you tap No thanks, nothing is sent. You can change this at any time in Settings > Usage Data.
  • If you agree, we record gameplay events, such as stages cleared, recruit pulls, loot claims, Ascensions, Arena battles and CheddarCombs floors, using Unity Analytics. Unity's SDK may also collect technical information such as device type, operating system, app version, language, approximate location (country or region) from your IP address, and session information.

Information you choose to share

  • If you use Copy Debug Info in Settings, the game copies your app version, device, Player ID, stage and recent error messages to your clipboard. Nothing is sent unless you paste it into a message to us.

2. How we use it

  • To run the game, save your progress and let you recover it on a new device.
  • To run chat, Arena rankings and the moderation system, and to keep the game fair and safe.
  • To fix bugs and balance the game, using the analytics you agreed to share.
  • To answer your support requests.

We do not sell your personal information. The game currently shows no advertising and has no in-app purchases. If that changes, we will update this policy first.

3. Who handles your data

We use these service providers, who process data for us under their own privacy terms:

  • Unity Technologies (Authentication, Cloud Save, Cloud Code, Analytics, Vivox) — unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google (Google Play, Google Play Games Services) — policies.google.com/privacy

Moderators and developers at SkaRift can view an account's profile, cloud save backup and chat reports when needed to handle a report, investigate a problem or fix an account at your request.

Your data may be processed on servers outside your country, including in the United States.

4. Children

Idle Rats is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it. Chat is not intended for children.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Analytics: switch it off in Settings > Usage Data at any time.
  • Access or deletion: email us and include your Player ID (Settings > Copy Debug Info shows it). We will delete the cloud data linked to that Player ID, or send you a copy, within a reasonable time. Deleting the game from your device removes the local save; cloud data stays until you ask us to delete it.
  • Chat and profile: you can change your display name and avatar in Profile.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or object to our use of your data. Email us to use them.

6. Security and retention

We protect your save with encryption and Unity's secure services, but no system is perfectly secure. We keep game and profile data while your account is active, and delete it on request. Chat reports and moderation records are kept as long as needed to keep the game safe.

7. Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top changes when we do, and we will tell players in the game for significant changes.
